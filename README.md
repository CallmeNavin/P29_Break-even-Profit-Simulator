# P28_Break-even-Profit-Simulator

A Streamlit app to simulate break-even revenue, profit, and margin of safety

## Business Problem

Many business owners and analysts know their current revenue, variable costs, and fixed costs, but may not know:

* How much revenue is required to break even
* How profitable the current situation is
* How much buffer exists before the business starts losing money

This tool provides a quick calculation of key break-even metrics.

## Features

Input:

* Revenue
* Variable Cost
* Fixed Cost

Output:

* Contribution
* Contribution Margin
* Break-even Revenue
* Profit
* Margin of Safety
